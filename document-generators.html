What a trust document generator does

A trust document generator takes the decisions behind a trust — the parties, the property, the powers, the distribution terms — and assembles them into the formal documents that make the trust real. The value is not the typing. It is consistency: the names, dates, and terms entered once flow into every document in the package, so the indenture, the property schedule, and the ownership certificates agree with each other word for word. Mismatched names and conflicting terms between a trust's own documents are among the most common — and most avoidable — defects in self-built trusts. Below is every document in the Build A Trust 24 package, what it does, and who needs it.

The Ownership Records

Certificates of Beneficial Interest

Numbered certificates stating each beneficiary's units of beneficial interest, generated for printing on bond paper with the trust's seal — and recorded in the trust's register at issue, so the certificate in a beneficiary's hands and the ledger in the trustee's vault always match.

Who needs it: any trust that divides ownership into units — families splitting interests among children, and trusts holding business or real estate interests for multiple beneficiaries.

How Full Access generates the package

The process is deliberately plain. Step one: work through the Trust Builder — name the trust, enter the grantor, trustee, and beneficiaries, choose the terms, and list the property. Step two: the generators assemble the indenture agreement and the certificates from those answers in one pass. Step three: review every document on screen, print the certificates on bond paper, and sign and notarize the indenture. Step four: fund the trust against the property schedule — the step that turns documents into a working structure — and file everything in the trust's vault records. Full Access is what turns the generators on; the free builder lets you structure the whole trust first, so you can see exactly what you are building before you commit.

What a generator cannot do

A trust document generator produces documents, not judgment. It cannot decide whether your trust should be revocable or irrevocable, weigh the tax treatment of your assets, or tell you whether a transfer you are planning would be a fraudulent conveyance — and no generated document erases taxes, judgments, or child support, or protects a trust funded on the eve of a claim. Generate the package, then have it reviewed by a qualified attorney in your state before you sign and fund. That review is cheaper than repairing a defective trust, every time.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the free builder and Full Access generation?

The free builder structures your trust — parties, terms, property — and shows you the framework. Full Access runs the generators: the complete indenture agreement and the certificates of beneficial interest, produced as finished documents from your builder answers, with the trust's records kept in the vault.

Are generated trust documents valid?

A trust's validity comes from the law of trusts — intent, property, parties, and proper execution — not from who typed the document. Generated documents that capture those elements and are correctly signed and funded create real trusts. Validity is fact- and state-specific, which is why review before execution is part of the process we recommend, not an optional extra.

Can the generator produce documents for a trust I already have?

The package is generated from a builder session, so the cleanest path for an existing trust is to rebuild its structure in the builder — which also gives you a consistency check against your current paperwork. Amending an existing indenture follows the amendment method written into that indenture itself.

What paper should the certificates be printed on?

Bond paper — the heavier, watermarked stock traditionally used for certificates and legal instruments. The generated certificates are formatted for it, with the seal position marked. The indenture itself prints on standard letter stock; keep the signed original with the trust's records.

Does the generator file anything with the state or a court?

No — and for a private express trust, that is correct. The indenture is a private instrument. The filings a trust may need sit elsewhere: deeds recorded when real estate moves into the trust, and a tax ID and returns for an irrevocable trust. The generator's job ends at correct, complete documents; the funding steps are yours, guided by the property schedule.
