2 · Run It Like a Trust Fund Manager

This is the manager’s desk. Run One Month pushes money through the Waterfall Method exactly as the main site teaches it — nothing skips a tier. Every move is written into the playground ledger below, the way a trustee’s accounting records it in both sets of books.

1
Tier 1 · Money Comes In at the Operating LevelEach operating business collects its monthly revenue into its own account. No trust money and no personal money is mixed in.
2
Tier 2 · The Operating Business Pays Its Own Way FirstExpenses, payroll, insurance, and taxes come out, and a working reserve stays behind. Only what is left is profit available to move.
3
Tier 3 · Profit Flows Up to the Holding CompanyEach business distributes its net profit to its owner — the holding company (or directly to the trust when there is no holding layer).
4
Tier 4 · The Holding Company Distributes to the TrustThe holding company passes what it received up to its owner, the trust. The trustee receives it as trust income and logs it.
5
Tier 5 · The Trustee Distributes Under the IndentureLast tier, on purpose: the trustee distributes to the beneficiaries according to their units. Every distribution is logged with its date, amount, and balance.

3 · Scan the Structure — Can It Legally Operate?

The Trust Fund Scanner checks the structure you built against the same rules the main site teaches: the trust type has to exist under the state you picked, the people have to be named, the units have to add up, and the trust has to actually be funded — an unfunded trust operates nothing. Each check comes back PASS or REVIEW with the reason in plain language.

Scanner disclaimer: The Trust Fund Scanner is an educational checklist, not a legal determination. It checks your structure against general rules and this site’s state notes — it cannot see your facts, your creditors, your tax picture, or your state’s full statute. Only a licensed attorney in your state can say whether a structure is legal for you. If you do not have the knowledge of a lawyer, consult a lawyer before implementing your trust.

4 · Implement It For Real
